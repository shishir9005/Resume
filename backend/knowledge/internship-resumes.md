# Resumes for Internship Applications

## Section order for students
A common order for students: Contact → Summary (optional) → Education → Projects → Experience → Skills → Awards or Activities. Put Education near the top while you are still studying. Move Experience above Projects once you have a relevant internship.

## Education details
Include university, degree, major, and expected graduation date ("Expected Jun 2027"). Add GPA if it is strong (for example 3.5/4.0 or higher, or top 20% of the class). List three to five relevant courses only if they match the role, such as "Data Structures, Databases, Machine Learning". Include notable scholarships or rankings.

## When you have little experience
Lean on projects, hackathons, open-source contributions, research assistant work, competitions (ACM ICPC, Kaggle, math modeling contests), and teaching assistant roles. Part-time jobs outside tech are still worth one line if they show responsibility, but keep them short.

## Activities and leadership
Student organizations, clubs, and volunteering show initiative. Write them like jobs: a role, an organization, and a bullet with a result, for example "Organized a 120-person coding workshop with 3 speakers".

## Keep it to one page
Recruiters spend well under a minute on a first read. A tight one-page resume with three strong projects beats two pages of everything you have done. Remove high-school details once you are in university, unless they are major national awards.
