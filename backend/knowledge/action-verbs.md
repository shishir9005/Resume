# Action Verbs

## Why the first word matters
Recruiters skim the first two or three words of each bullet. A specific action verb tells them immediately what you did. The ATS Resume Scorer also counts strong action verbs as part of the Content Quality score, so bullets that begin with weak phrases cost points.

## Weak openers to replace
Avoid starting bullets with "Responsible for", "Worked on", "Helped with", "Assisted in", "Involved in", "Participated in", "Was part of", or "Tasked with". These describe a duty, not an achievement. Replace them with what you actually did: "Responsible for the database" becomes "Designed the PostgreSQL schema for 14 tables".

## Verbs for building and engineering
Built, Developed, Engineered, Implemented, Designed, Architected, Deployed, Integrated, Automated, Refactored, Migrated, Containerized, Optimized, Debugged, Prototyped, Shipped.

## Verbs for data, research and analysis
Analyzed, Modeled, Trained, Fine-tuned, Evaluated, Benchmarked, Visualized, Forecasted, Measured, Validated, Researched, Identified, Quantified.

## Verbs for leadership and collaboration
Led, Coordinated, Mentored, Organized, Presented, Proposed, Negotiated, Facilitated, Collaborated with, Represented, Launched, Initiated.

## Verbs for improvement and impact
Reduced, Increased, Improved, Accelerated, Streamlined, Simplified, Cut, Saved, Raised, Expanded, Resolved, Eliminated. Pair these with a number whenever possible: "Reduced build time by 50%".
